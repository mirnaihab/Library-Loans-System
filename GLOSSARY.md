# Library Loans

Library members borrow and return books; the library enforces fair-use rules on who can borrow what.

## Language

**Member**:
A person registered with the library who can borrow books.

**Loan**:
The record of one member borrowing one book, from the day it is borrowed until it is returned.

**Active loan**:
A loan whose book has not yet been returned, whether or not it is overdue.
_Avoid_: On loan, borrowed (as separate concepts), checked out

**Borrowing limit**:
The maximum number of active loans one member may hold at the same time; the same for every member.
_Avoid_: Slot, quota, allowance

**Due date**:
The day by which a loan's book should be returned.

**Overdue**:
Describes an active loan whose due date has passed.
_Avoid_: Late (for a loan still out)

**Late fee**:
A charge created when a book is returned after its due date; one per late return.
_Avoid_: Fine, penalty

**Outstanding fees**:
The total of a member's late fees that have not yet been paid.
_Avoid_: Balance, debt
